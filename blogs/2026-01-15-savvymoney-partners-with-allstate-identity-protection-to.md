Integration enables financial partners to offer comprehensive identity theft recovery support directly within digital banking platforms. DUBLIN, Calif. (January 15, 2026) — SavvyMoney, a leading provider of integrated credit score, financial wellness and lending solutions, has partnered with Allstate Identity Protection to bring identity restoration services directly into the SavvyMoney Credit Score experience.
