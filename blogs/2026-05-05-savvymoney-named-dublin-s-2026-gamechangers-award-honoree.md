DUBLIN, Calif. (April 28, 2026) — SavvyMoney has been selected to represent the City of Dublin, California at the 2026 #GameChangers Awards, hosted by Innovation Tri-Valley. The annual #GameChangers Awards recognize standout companies, founders, and leaders who are shaping the future of the Tri-Valley’s innovation ecosystem.
